Chapter 2 · Who joined the record

The dip is the athletes

Colour in everyone whose Wikidata description is a sports career. Among people born before 1960 they never make up more than of a decade. Among people born since 1990 they are .

An athlete is notable young — a roster spot or an Olympic entry is enough for Wikidata — while most careers take decades to be written up. And Stanford's athletes are overwhelmingly American-born — so as they fill the youngest columns, the share born abroad sinks.
